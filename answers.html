Common questions

Is an annual test the same as a repair?

No. The annual visit tests the assembly. A repair or rebuild is extra work after a fail. Replacement is a different job. Ask which of those is quoted.

Who receives the San Antonio test report?

SAWS has contracted with BSI Online to send many due notices and to receive filings. Ask the tester whether they file the report and whether filing is included. This site is not SAWS or BSI.

Who licenses backflow testers in Texas?

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality licenses backflow prevention assembly testers. Ask for the number on the report. This site is not TCEQ.

Why is a repair quote higher than the annual test?

A passing test is one service. Kits, rebuilds, and new assemblies are different work. Ask for the test result first. This site does not set prices.

Do I have to replace an assembly that failed once?

Ask whether a repair kit can restore it versus replacement. Get the fail reason in writing and confirm the retest and refile.
